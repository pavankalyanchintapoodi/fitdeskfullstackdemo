## How App Works:

Every time you add a new member:
  - Welcome email sent to their email (2 min)
  - Welcome SMS sent to their phone (10-20 sec, if Twilio)
  - Personalized with their name and plan
  - Automatically logged in database

Daily at 9 AM:
  - System checks all members
  - Finds anyone expiring tomorrow
  - Sends renewal reminder to member
  - Sends alert to owner

Anytime you want:
  - Go to Notifications tab
  - Click "Run Check Now"
  - View all notification history
